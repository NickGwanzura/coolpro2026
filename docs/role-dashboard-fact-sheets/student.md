# Student Dashboard: Fact Sheet

**Page:** `/dashboard` (titled "My Dashboard")  |  **Role in system:** `student`

## Purpose
A learner home: see the courses you are taking, how far you have got, and how your exams are going.

## Who it's for
Learners enrolled in HVAC-R training who are not yet registered technicians. Students register themselves from the Join page; an administrator approves them after checking their student ID.

## KPI cards (top row)
All four are current totals and are not affected by the period filter.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| My Courses | Approved courses you have enrolled in; the note shows how many are available in total |
| Exams Passed | Your graded exams that met the course pass mark; the note shows how many you have submitted |
| Awaiting Grading | Exams you submitted that your trainer has not marked yet |
| Available Courses | Approved courses you have not enrolled in yet |

## Panels
- **My courses.** Each course you are enrolled in, with a progress bar (modules completed out of total) and your exam state: not taken, awaiting grading, passed, or not passed.
- **Available Courses.** Approved courses open for enrolment, with a link into the Learning Hub.

## How learning works
- Click **Start course** in the Learning Hub to enrol. Enrolment unlocks the course materials and its exam.
- Tick modules off as you finish them. Your progress is saved to your account, so it follows you to another device and your trainer can see it.
- Each course has a pass mark. You get three attempts at the exam, and once you pass you are done. Waiting for grading stops you submitting another attempt for the same course.

## Quick actions
My Learning, Certifications, WhatGas Registry, Safety Center.

## Menu items available
Dashboard, Learning Hub, Safety Center, WhatGas + Risk Engine, Refrigerant Catalogue, Certification, plus the Emergency Mode button.

## Notifications
You are notified when an exam is graded, with your score, and when your account is first approved.

## Notes for the fact sheet
- Students cannot see field, supplier, rewards or admin pages.

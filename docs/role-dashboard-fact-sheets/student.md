# Student Dashboard: Fact Sheet

**Page:** `/dashboard` (titled "My Dashboard")  |  **Role in system:** `student`  |  **Test login:** student@zimhvacregistry.org

## Purpose
A simple learner home: browse approved courses, take assessments, and view issued certificates.

## Who it's for
Learners enrolled in HVAC-R training who are not yet registered technicians.

## KPI cards (top row)
| Card | What it shows | How it's counted |
|---|---|---|
| Available Courses | Courses open for enrollment | Courses with approved status in the catalogue |

*This is the only KPI card on the student dashboard.*

## Quick actions
- **My Learning**: enrolled courses and exams
- **Certifications**: view issued certificates
- **WhatGas Registry**: refrigerant reference lookup
- **Safety Center**: guidance and safety resources

## Dashboard panels
- **Available Courses**: list of approved courses with a link into the Learning Hub

## Learning Hub summary (`/learn`, learner view)
- Welcome banner with the student's name
- Counters: Available courses, Assessments submitted, Assessments passed
- Next course suggestion, a "Learn, assess, certify" path, Recent assessments list

## Menu items available
Dashboard, Learning Hub, Safety Center, WhatGas + Risk Engine, Refrigerant Catalogue, Certification, plus the Emergency Mode button.

## Notes for the fact sheet
- Students cannot see field, supplier, rewards or admin pages.
- Students can join through the public form at `/join/student`.
